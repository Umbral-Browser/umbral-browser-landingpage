# Umbral community code of conduct

We want people to collaborate safely and disagree constructively. Treat participants respectfully regardless of experience, background, identity, beliefs or personal circumstances.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes.
- Keep technical reports useful and relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy, consent and others' time.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal insults, publishing private information and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable. Technical or political disagreement does not excuse these behaviors.

## Scope and moderation

This applies in Umbral project spaces and when officially representing the project. Maintainers may request corrections, remove content, warn participants, or limit participation proportionately. Serious threats or privacy violations can justify immediate restrictions. Explain decisions where appropriate without exposing sensitive information.

## Reporting

GitHub's reporting tools are available for abusive content on its platform. A dedicated private Umbral moderation contact is **not yet configured** and must be established before a wider community launch. Do not post personal or sensitive incident details in public issues and do not contact Zen's team about Umbral moderation.

This is a project-specific draft for review, not a claim that a staffed moderation service or response deadline already exists.
